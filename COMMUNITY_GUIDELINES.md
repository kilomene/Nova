# Nova Community Guidelines

Last updated: 2026-10-16

These are the rules. Break them and there are consequences — no surprises.

## 1. Nudity: zero tolerance

Nova does not allow nudity. Period.

- **Uploads:** Any nude image or video upload is immediately flagged and you
  get **1 warning strike**. Our on-device scanner checks uploads before they
  publish; borderline cases go to human review.
- **Livestreams:** Nudity on a livestream = **instant account ban**. No
  strikes, no warnings. The stream is terminated immediately and the case
  goes to review.

This is our most important community rule.

## 2. The 3-strike system

Strikes are counted per account:

- **Strike 1:** You get an in-app notification + push telling you exactly
  what happened: "This is strike 1 of 3."
- **Strike 2:** Same again: "This is strike 2 of 3." Final warning.
- **Strike 3:** Your account is **automatically suspended** per these
  guidelines. Your case goes to the admin review queue, where the
  suspension is either upheld or lifted after human review.

You are notified at every strike. No silent punishments.

## 3. Authenticity: no fakes, no impersonation

- Fake accounts are removed.
- Impersonating another person is a strike offense. Report impersonation
  and it goes straight to the review queue.
- Bot accounts created by Nova's own agent systems are labeled **BOT** in
  the app — they never pretend to be human.

## 4. Fraud

Scamming other users is a ban offense. Fraud reports capture evidence
(transaction records, chat history, amounts, timestamps) and are
investigated. Where the evidence supports it, Nova files cases with law
enforcement on the victim's behalf (EFCC, FBI IC3, Action Fraud, CAFC,
and others by jurisdiction).

## 5. Copyright

Only upload audio you have the right to use. Every upload with attached
audio requires you to confirm: **"I confirm I have the right to use this
audio."** That confirmation is logged. Rights holders can request
takedowns through the report flow (copyright category).

## 6. Spam & harassment

Comment spam (flooding, identical repetition, link/mention abuse) is
auto-flagged; repeat offenders collect strikes. Harassment reports are
reviewed like every other report — **no report is ever auto-dismissed.**

## 7. Sellers

Selling on Nova requires identity verification (government ID +
country-specific identity number + address). Unverified accounts cannot
list products. Buyers need only a normal account.

## 8. Appeals

Suspended or banned? Your case is in the review queue. Decisions are made
by a human (or the support agent) — upheld or lifted, with a note.

---

*Shown at signup and in Settings. By using Nova you agree to these rules.*
